Advanced Conditional Forms

Select a grammar point to present to the class.

Supposing / Imagine

We often use supposing or imagine instead of if in spoken situations. This suggests an act of imagination.

Examples

  • Supposing you could go anywhere? Where would you go?
  • Imagine you won the lottery. What would you buy first?

2. you had to move to another country tomorrow. How would you feel?

Unless

Unless tends to be used more in real conditionals and means if not. We don't use unless in questions.

Examples

  • Unless we change the way we think about work, we'll never be able to face the challenges of the 21st century.

Check your understanding

Rewrite the sentences using unless.

1. If you don't study, you will fail the test.
→ you study, you will fail the test.
2. I won't go to the party if she doesn't invite me.
→ I won't go to the party she invites me.

Provided (that) / As long as

Provided (that) / As long as has a similar sense of reservation as unless but means only if.

Examples

  • Provided / As long as the stadium is finished, the games will go ahead as planned.

Check your understanding

1. You can borrow my car, but only if you drive carefully.
→ You can borrow my car you drive carefully.
2. We will go hiking tomorrow if the weather is good.
→ We will go hiking tomorrow the weather is good.

In case

In case is used to suggest readiness for a possible future development.

Examples

  • We'll take snacks in case we get hungry.

Check your understanding

1. Take an umbrella because it might rain later.
→ Take an umbrella it rains later.
2. I'll write down his number because I might forget it.
→ I'll write down his number I forget it.

Even if

Even if talks about a result that will happen whether or not the condition is true.

Examples

  • We have to eat outside even if the weather is bad.

Check your understanding

1. The match will continue regardless of whether it snows.
→ The match will continue it snows.
2. She won't forgive him, regardless of how much he apologizes.
→ She won't forgive him he apologizes.
